## 🌈 Prism Stampede

**Prism Stampede** is a colorful arcade-puzzle where you guide a cloud-riding unicorn, launch magical Sparks through numbered prisms, match colors to free sealed Cloudlings, rescue lost unicorns, and defeat Storm Guardians before they reach the danger line.

### 🎮 Controls

- **Mouse/Touch:** Drag and release to aim and fire
- **Keyboard:** Use `A`/`D` or the arrow keys to aim
- **Fire:** Press `Space` or `Enter`
- **Fast-forward:** Press `F`
- **Restart:** Press `R`
- **Help:** Press `H` or `?`

### ✨ Features

- 15 levels
- Numbered Prism Routes and color-matching attacks
- Sealed Cloudlings, Starbursts, rescues, and Guardians
- Three-star scoring
- Responsive desktop and touch controls
- Persistent progress
